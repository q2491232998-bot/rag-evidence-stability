# Third-party sources

- MuSiQue metric code and reference documentation are retained unmodified under
  `implementation/references/musique_official/`, with their original `LICENSE`.
  Source: https://github.com/stonybrooknlp/musique .
- HotpotQA questions: https://hotpotqa.github.io/ . Study question and answer
  text is not redistributed in this package.
- BEIR HotpotQA corpus: https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir . Corpus passages and
  Wikipedia source text are not redistributed. IDs and hashes refer to the
  frozen corpus snapshot described in the source manifests.
- Qwen3 models: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B and
  https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B . No weights are redistributed.
- BGE and E5 retrieval assets are described in `protocols/retrieval_assets.json`.
  No index or embedding weights are redistributed.

Acquire external assets from their respective providers under the applicable
terms. The original-code and derived-data licenses in this repository do not
replace any third-party license. Source revisions and hashes are recorded in
the frozen implementation and protocol manifests.
